# Part A: Two core feature suggestions

> NOTE: Replace or sharpen the "what I noticed" lines with your own observations from using
> https://app.getcoy.com (OTP login, adding a crush, email verification, invite link, extra-slots screen).

## 1. Earn extra slots by inviting friends

**What:** When someone joins through your invite link and finishes verification, both of you get one extra crush slot. Show a small progress line on the extra-slots screen, for example "Invite 2 friends to unlock a free slot".

**Why it's needed:**
- Coy only works when the crush is also on Coy, so growth is the product. Right now an invite link exists, but nothing rewards using it.
- The extra-slots screen is a paywall moment. People who don't want to pay at that point have no next step, so many will drop off. Earning a slot gives them one, and it brings in new users.
- Referred users arrive with a real reason to be there (a friend or a crush invited them), so they are more likely to stay than users from ads.
- Privacy stays intact: the reward only says "a friend joined", and never says who someone added.

**Guardrails:** Count a referral only after phone OTP and email verification are both done, and cap free slots, to stop fake accounts.

 hi") and send one reminder after about 24 hours if neither person has written.

**Why it's needed:**
- A mutual reveal is a nervous moment. Both people already know the other likes them, and still nobody wants to go first. That can leave matches with no conversation.
- A ready-made opener removes the pressure of the first message, which is the biggest barrier right after a reveal.
- Conversations are what make users come back. A match with no chat gives them no reason to return.
- The reminder is neutral and goes to both people. It never says who liked whom first.
